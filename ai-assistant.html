📞 Recruiting & Follow-Up
🎯 Recruiting Script Generator
Writes a personalized recruiting pitch based on a prospect's background
I'm recruiting for Dynasty Builders Academy, a PHP/HGI life insurance agency. Write me a natural, non-pushy recruiting script for this prospect: Name: [NAME] Occupation: [JOB] Key motivation: [WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT — money, time freedom, helping others, etc.] The script should: 1. Open with a genuine connection to their current situation 2. Position the opportunity around what they care about most 3. Use the 3-question soft close: "Are you open to looking at something on the side? Do you keep your options open? Great — I'm not even sure if you'd qualify, but can I share what we're doing?" 4. End with a specific ask for a 20-minute call
💬 Follow-Up Message Writer
Writes follow-up texts and emails after appointments and calls
Write a follow-up message for this situation: Person: [NAME] What happened: [DESCRIBE THE CALL/MEETING — e.g. "we had a great RI, they're interested but want to think about it"] Their main concern: [OBJECTION OR HESITATION] Next step I want: [e.g. "schedule a second call", "get them to the BOM", "have them complete the application"] Write: 1. A text message (under 160 characters, warm and personal) 2. An email version (3-4 sentences, professional but friendly) 3. A voicemail script (under 30 seconds) Keep the tone warm, never pushy. Reference something specific from our conversation.
