Golf Score Board Settings

Dynasty Builder of the Week is calculated automatically from each agent's highest total score across all 4 categories wins. 1st place earns the most points (= field size), each lower rank earns one fewer. Not entering a category scores 0. Best all-around performer takes the crown.

If an agent doesn't place in a category, they're not scored for that category (only agents who appear in at least one category are eligible).
