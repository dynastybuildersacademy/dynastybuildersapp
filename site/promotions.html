👤 Agent Info
One row per agent: agentId, recruits, pts, apps, subPts, paidPts, licensed, fastStartDone — agentId is the login ID from Roster Admin (e.g. obi001). Leave a column blank if you don't have that number yet. fastStartDone lists completed Fast Start School steps separated by ; (not commas): spouse, plan, top25, ft3, finance, life, event, sprint. First row can be a header — it's detected automatically.
Note: subPts/paidPts entered here are overwritten by Client Info totals below, once a client record exists for that agent.
💰 Client Info
One row per client policy: agentId, clientName, submittedPts, paidPts, status — status is optional (e.g. Submitted, Paid, Pending). Each agent's subPts/paidPts totals on their card are the sum of their client rows here, so you get both the roll-up number and an auditable list of what it's made of.
The Captains System
The Captain's System assists associates in the Base Shop with confirming their guests and following up the next day.
How It Works: 10 → 3 → 1
If you have a guest for the BOM, notify the Base Shop Coordinator with the guest's name and number. The coordinator confirms attendance and follows up.

100 invitations → 30 people showing up → 10 people getting started
250 invitations → 75 people showing up → 25 people getting started

If you have 10+ invites to any given BOM, you are considered a "Captain".
How to Use the Captain System
1
Email your list of invites to your office Base Shop Coordinator a day before or by noon the day of BOM.
2
Make sure your guests were called by the Base Shop Coordinator or yourself to confirm their attendance.
3
Follow up if a guest doesn't show up. They are now on a systematized follow-up process. Either you, the office assistant, or the Base Shop Coordinator can follow up for an in-person interview.
Most people using the Captain System put the law of numbers in their favor and run a more predictable business for recruiting new agents and getting clients.

If someone does not get started or show up, focus on following up until they specify they are truly not interested.

This system allows you to keep track of individuals who have visited the office and creates a follow-up driven business.
