⬆ Power vs Force — Levels of Consciousness
A Christian self-development framework inspired by the Map of Consciousness model. Understand where you operate today — and how the work of Christ moves you upward.
The Three Tiers
TIER 1 — Contracted States (Force)
😔 Shame & Guilt — hiding, self-blame 😶 Apathy & Grief — withdrawal, numbness 😰 Fear & Desire — anxious control, craving 😠 Anger & Pride — blame, need to win
Scripture: Genesis 3 · Psalm 51 · 1 Kings 19 · Genesis 4
TIER 2 — The Turning Point
⚡ Courage — facing life, acting despite fear
"Be strong and courageous" — Joshua 1:9 · David & Goliath (1 Samuel 17)
TIER 3 — Expanded States (Power)
🕊 Willingness & Acceptance — openness, letting go 🧠 Reason — clear thinking, seeing patterns 💛 Love, Joy & Peace — warmth, deep stillness
1 Corinthians 13 · Galatians 5:22-23 · Isaiah 1:18
✝ How the Work of Jesus Moves You Upward
This model treats movement between tiers not as mere self-help, but as something scripture describes as being done to a person through Christ — not just self-generated effort.
The cross answers guilt, shame, and pride. "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come" (2 Corinthians 5:17).
The resurrection answers apathy and grief. Believers are born again to a living hope — grief is given an end point, not just a coping mechanism (1 Peter 1:3, Revelation 21:4).
The Spirit opens courage. "God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control" (2 Timothy 1:7). The disciples — hiding in John 20, then preaching boldly in Acts 4. Same men. Different level.
Renewal moves you toward love, joy, and peace. "Be transformed by the renewal of your mind" (Romans 12:2). Love, joy, and peace are fruit the Spirit produces — not gritted out by effort (Galatians 5:22-23).
📊 Self-Assessment (17 Questions)

Rate each statement 0–3 for the past 2–4 weeks (0 = Rarely · 1 = Sometimes · 2 = Often · 3 = Almost always). Three sections: Contracted States (8 items) · Courage Threshold (2 items) · Expanded States (7 items).

Section A — Contracted (max 24):
I feel unworthy or deeply flawed · I feel undeserving of forgiveness · I believe nothing in my life will change · I experience a persistent sense of loss · I feel anxious about future outcomes · I find myself craving or grasping compulsively · I feel resentment toward others for my situation · I need to be seen as right or to win arguments

Section B — Courage Threshold (max 6):
I am able to face problems directly, even when it's uncomfortable · I act on important things even when I'm uncertain of the outcome

Section C — Expanded (max 21):
I can release control over outcomes I can't determine · I am open to changing my approach when needed · I can forgive others without needing a full resolution · I can see the logic or pattern in a difficult situation · I feel genuine warmth toward the people around me · I experience contentment without needing anything to change first · I have a settled sense that things are fundamentally okay
Score: Section A ≥14 → Contracted baseline dominates. Section B ≥4 → Courage threshold crossed. Identify your highest-scoring A item (your active edge) or lowest-scoring C item to target your practice.

✨ Power Affirmations — All Areas of Life

Speak these daily — morning is best. These are declarations of identity, not requests. Say them as present truth.

🙏 Spiritual

I am not trying to reach God — I am a son/daughter in whom He is well pleased. My spirit is alive, alert, and responsive to the Holy Spirit. I walk in the fruit of the Spirit naturally — love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control flow from who I am, not what I do. I am being transformed daily by the renewal of my mind.

Romans 8:16 · Galatians 5:22-23 · Romans 12:2

🧠 Mental & Emotional

I have the mind of Christ — clarity, wisdom, and discernment are available to me in every situation. I do not operate from fear, anxiety, or scarcity. I feel my emotions fully and move through them cleanly. I am not controlled by what happens to me — I choose my response. My mind is sharp, my thoughts are ordered, and I return to peace quickly.

1 Corinthians 2:16 · 2 Timothy 1:7 · Philippians 4:7

💍 Marriage

My marriage is a covenant, not a contract. I love my spouse as Christ loved the church — sacrificially, not conditionally. I see my partner's greatness even when they cannot see it themselves. I bring peace, security, and warmth into our home. We grow stronger through every season, and what we are building together is greater than either of us could build alone.

Ephesians 5:25 · Ecclesiastes 4:9-12 · Proverbs 31

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family

My family is a legacy, not just a household. My children see in me what they can become. I lead my home with love, structure, and faith — not perfection. We are a team, and every member of my family is growing into their God-given identity. The atmosphere of my home is one of safety, joy, and possibility.

Psalm 128:3 · Proverbs 22:6 · Joshua 24:15

🎯 Career

I am excellent at what I do and my work creates real value for others. I am promoted because I develop people and produce results simultaneously. I show up with full commitment to my craft — I am always growing, always learning, and always raising the standard. My gifts make room for me, and doors open because of how I serve.

Proverbs 18:16 · Colossians 3:23-24 · Daniel 1:20

⚡ Business

My business is built on service, not sales. I solve real problems for real people and prosperity follows naturally. I build systems, not just results — what I create runs even when I am not in the room. My business is a vehicle for generational impact, not just personal income. I think in decades while I execute daily.

Deuteronomy 8:18 · Proverbs 16:3 · Luke 16:10

💰 Financial

Money flows to me because I create value and serve well. I am a faithful steward — I manage what I have been given with integrity and wisdom. I am debt-free in direction, building wealth with purpose, and generating income that outlasts my effort. I give generously because I operate from abundance, not scarcity. My financial decisions reflect who I am becoming, not who I was.

Deuteronomy 28:12 · Proverbs 13:11 · Luke 6:38

🤝 Relationships

I attract high-quality relationships because I am a high-quality person. I am loyal, honest, and genuinely interested in the growth of those around me. I do not drain people — I add energy to every room I enter. I set boundaries from love, not fear. I resolve conflict quickly and do not carry offense. My inner circle is a reflection of my values and my aspirations.

Proverbs 27:17 · 1 Corinthians 15:33 · Romans 12:18

🏗 Team & Partners

I lead from strength, not from need. My team is inspired by who I am before they are instructed by what I say. I recognize the greatness in every person I lead and I call it out before they see it themselves. I build up — I do not tear down. I am the kind of leader that people would run through walls for, because they know I am for them. Together we accomplish what none of us could reach alone.

Mark 10:43-44 · Proverbs 11:14 · Nehemiah 2:18

"I receive God's love and forgiveness,
and I walk in newness of life."

"Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come." — 2 Corinthians 5:17

HOW TO USE: Read aloud each morning — ideally before screens. Start with the area where you need the most growth. Say it slowly, as truth. These are not positive thinking — they are identity declarations that rewire the operating level from which you live and lead.
