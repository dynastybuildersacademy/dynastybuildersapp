⬆ Power vs Force — Levels of Consciousness
A Christian self-development framework inspired by the Map of Consciousness model. Understand where you operate today — and how the work of Christ moves you upward.
The Three Tiers
TIER 1 — Contracted States (Force)
😔 Shame & Guilt — hiding, self-blame 😶 Apathy & Grief — withdrawal, numbness 😰 Fear & Desire — anxious control, craving 😠 Anger & Pride — blame, need to win
Scripture: Genesis 3 · Psalm 51 · 1 Kings 19 · Genesis 4
TIER 2 — The Turning Point
⚡ Courage — facing life, acting despite fear
"Be strong and courageous" — Joshua 1:9 · David & Goliath (1 Samuel 17)
TIER 3 — Expanded States (Power)
🕊 Willingness & Acceptance — openness, letting go 🧠 Reason — clear thinking, seeing patterns 💛 Love, Joy & Peace — warmth, deep stillness
1 Corinthians 13 · Galatians 5:22-23 · Isaiah 1:18
✝ How the Work of Jesus Moves You Upward
This model treats movement between tiers not as mere self-help, but as something scripture describes as being done to a person through Christ — not just self-generated effort.
The cross answers guilt, shame, and pride. "If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away; behold, the new has come" (2 Corinthians 5:17).
The resurrection answers apathy and grief. Believers are born again to a living hope — grief is given an end point, not just a coping mechanism (1 Peter 1:3, Revelation 21:4).
The Spirit opens courage. "God gave us a spirit not of fear but of power and love and self-control" (2 Timothy 1:7). The disciples — hiding in John 20, then preaching boldly in Acts 4. Same men. Different level.
Renewal moves you toward love, joy, and peace. "Be transformed by the renewal of your mind" (Romans 12:2). Love, joy, and peace are fruit the Spirit produces — not gritted out by effort (Galatians 5:22-23).
📊 Self-Assessment (17 Questions)

Rate each statement 0–3 for the past 2–4 weeks (0 = Rarely · 1 = Sometimes · 2 = Often · 3 = Almost always). Three sections: Contracted States (8 items) · Courage Threshold (2 items) · Expanded States (7 items).

Section A — Contracted (max 24):
I feel unworthy or deeply flawed · I feel undeserving of forgiveness · I believe nothing in my life will change · I experience a persistent sense of loss · I feel anxious about future outcomes · I find myself craving or grasping compulsively · I feel resentment toward others for my situation · I need to be seen as right or to win arguments

Section B — Courage Threshold (max 6):
I am able to face problems directly, even when it's uncomfortable · I act on important things even when I'm uncertain of the outcome

Section C — Expanded (max 21):
I can release control over outcomes I can't determine · I am open to changing my approach when needed · I can forgive others without needing a full resolution · I can see the logic or pattern in a difficult situation · I feel genuine warmth toward the people around me · I experience contentment without needing anything to change first · I have a settled sense that things are fundamentally okay
Score: Section A ≥14 → Contracted baseline dominates. Section B ≥4 → Courage threshold crossed. Identify your highest-scoring A item (your active edge) or lowest-scoring C item to target your practice.

I have the mind of Christ — clarity, wisdom, and discernment are available to me in every situation. I do not operate from fear, anxiety, or scarcity. Money flows to me because I create value and serve well. I am a faithful steward — I manage what I have been given with integrity and wisdom.
