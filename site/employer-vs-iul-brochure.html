Most working Americans have group life insurance through their employer or union — and most assume it's enough. But here's the truth: group term provides only temporary, income-replacement coverage with zero accumulation. The moment you leave your job, retire, or the union renegotiates, that coverage can disappear. An IUL gives you something entirely different: permanent protection + a tax-advantaged savings engine that grows with the market and never drops below zero.

Which Is Right for You?

🏢
Keep Your Employer Plan If…
  • You need maximum coverage at minimum cost
  • You have no medical history and guaranteed issue matters
  • You want union-specific perks like strike premium waivers
  • You're in your 20s and building emergency savings first
  • Your employer covers 100% of the premium cost
💡
Upgrade to an IUL If…
  • You want coverage that follows you regardless of job
  • You want tax-free supplemental retirement income
  • You're worried about long-term care or chronic illness costs
  • You want to leave a tax-free legacy for your family
  • Your employer coverage ends at retirement — right when you need it most
