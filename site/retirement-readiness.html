Where should your money work?

Every account type is a different combination of liquidity, risk, tax treatment, and income.

Fixed
Predictability

A stated or declared rate for a period of time. Lower direct market exposure; terms and liquidity vary by product.

Variable
Market Exposure

Value is tied to investment subaccounts. Offers upside potential, but account value can decline with the market.

Indexed
Protected Growth Potential

Interest is credited using an index formula. You're not directly invested in the index; caps, participation rates, or spreads may apply.

Protect. Grow. Create income.

A simple guide to fixed indexed annuities — designed to show how one might fit into a retirement strategy.

1. Protection

Your contract is not directly invested in the stock market. Negative index performance generally does not create a negative index credit, although withdrawals, surrender charges, and other contract charges can reduce value.

2. Growth Potential

Interest may be credited based on the performance of a market index. Caps, participation rates, spreads, and crediting periods determine how much interest is credited.

3. Retirement Income

Annuities can be used to create a predictable income stream. Lifetime income options may be available through annuitization or optional income riders.
