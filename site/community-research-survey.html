How can financial services better serve your community?

We're listening before we teach. This short survey helps Dynasty Builders Academy understand what Greater LA families want to learn about money, life insurance, retirement, and financial services — and whether financial terminology, advertising, and access feel like they actually work for your community.

Takes about 5–8 minutes. Every question except your name is optional. There's a short, clearly-marked section at the end about careers in financial services — you can skip it entirely.

We educate first. We recommend second. This is research, not a sales pitch — your answers won't be used to decide what to sell you.
