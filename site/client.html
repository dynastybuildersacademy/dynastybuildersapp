Why Living Benefits Change Everything

Modern life insurance pays out while you're still alive — not just when you pass. These three riders are built into many policies at no extra cost.

❤️

Critical Illness

Access 25–100% of your death benefit if diagnosed with heart attack, stroke, cancer, or other qualifying illness. No receipts required — spend it how you need.

♿

Chronic Illness

If you can no longer perform basic daily activities independently, accelerate your death benefit to fund long-term care — without a separate LTC policy.

🏁

Terminal Illness

Access 50–100% of your death benefit early if diagnosed terminal — check items off your bucket list and handle your affairs on your own terms.
