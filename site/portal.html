Referral Scripts

🗣️ Asking for Referrals After Close (The Gold Standard)

[After policy is delivered or just after application]

"[Client Name], I want to say thank you for trusting me with your family's protection. That means a lot.

The way my business grows is through people like you referring me to the people they care about.

I'm not asking you to sell anyone — I'm just asking you to connect me with one or two people who you think deserve to know what you now know.

Who comes to mind — maybe a family member, coworker, or close friend who has kids or a mortgage they haven't fully protected yet?"

📱 Text Message to Referral (First Outreach)

"Hi [Referral Name], my name is [Your Name]. Your [friend/coworker/family member] [Referrer Name] mentioned you and suggested I reach out.

I work with families on life insurance and financial protection — specifically the type that pays out for critical illness, not just death.

[Referrer Name] thought it might be worth a 15-minute conversation to at least see where you stand. Would you be open to a quick call this week?"

📞 Cold Referral Call Opener

"Hi, is this [Name]? [Yes] Great — my name is [Your Name], I'm a financial professional. Your [friend] [Referrer] suggested I give you a call.

Do you have about 60 seconds? [Yes]

I work with people to make sure their family would be financially okay if anything happened to them — specifically looking at coverage gaps most people don't know they have. [Referrer] just went through this process and wanted me to give you the same information.

I'm not here to sell you anything today — I'd just love to schedule 20 minutes to walk you through what we found for your friend and see if it applies to you. Fair enough?"

🔄 Referral Follow-Up (No Response)

"Hi [Name], just following up on my message from [X days] ago. I completely understand if now isn't the best time.

I just want to make sure I did right by [Referrer] who spoke so highly of you.

If you have 15 minutes this week or next, I'd love to share something that could be genuinely valuable for your family. No pressure — just a conversation.

Text me back 'YES' if you're open to it, and I'll send you a time that works."
