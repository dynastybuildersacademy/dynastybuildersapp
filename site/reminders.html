🔔 Team Reminders
📡 Delivery Method
Reminders are sent via Zapier. Connect your Zapier webhook below, then build a Zap that sends to your team's group chat, email, or SMS list.
⚡ Zapier Setup
1. Zapier → New Zap → Schedule by Zapier trigger → set your time
2. Add a Webhooks → GET step pointing to this page's reminder endpoint
3. Or use Webhooks → Catch Hook and click Send Test below
4. Connect to Gmail, GroupMe, Slack, Twilio SMS, or WhatsApp
