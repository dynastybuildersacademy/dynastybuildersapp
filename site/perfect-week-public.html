1The Four Levels of Your Why
Everybody has a why. The challenge is that most people never graduate from their initial why.

This mirrors Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs: if you're struggling to survive, you're not thinking about purpose. Once your core needs are met, you naturally move up toward belonging, esteem, and growth. Be honest about where you actually are right now — not where you think you should be.
