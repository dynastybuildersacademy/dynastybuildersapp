🪄 The Magic Wand Exercise — Brian Tracy

Imagine you could wave a magic wand and make your life perfect in every key area. If your life was completely ideal 5 years from now, what would it look like? Write it as if you're describing a movie of your future life — absolute clarity, no hedging.

What would you be doing? How much would you be earning? What level of success would you have attained?
What kind of lifestyle do you want to create for yourself and the people closest to you? Who would be there with you?
How would your health be different if it was perfect in every way?
How much money do you want to save and accumulate over your lifetime?
How would you spend an ideal day, week, or year? Where would you live?
Write your top goals daily in the present tense, starting with "I" — e.g. "I earn $10,000/month by December 2027" or "I am at my ideal weight."
Work backward from your ideal life to today, then take action on at least one thing immediately. What's the very next action you can take right now?
