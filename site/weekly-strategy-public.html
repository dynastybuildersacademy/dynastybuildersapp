2Create a Definite Weekly Aim

3Visualize the Result
Principles: Imagination + Autosuggestion + Faith. Close your eyes and mentally travel to the end of the week — the goal is already accomplished.
3BDream Life Image / Vision Board
Principle: Imagination + Faith + Autosuggestion — "Whatever the mind can clearly conceive and believe, it can achieve."
Take 2–5 minutes and answer these honestly. Your answers become the prompt for a Dream Life Image or Vision Board — a visual reminder of who you're becoming and what you're building.

4Turn the Vision Into an Organized Plan
Napoleon Hill Principle: Organized Planning — faith should produce action. Work backward from the result using proven activities.
5Break the Week Into Daily Action
Principle: Decision + Persistence — decide before the week begins, don't wake up each morning deciding what to do.
6Identify Your Inspired Action
"If I genuinely believed my goal was already possible, what action would I take today?"
7Build Faith Through Autosuggestion
Napoleon Hill Principle: Autosuggestion — read this every morning and evening.
My goal this week is ___________________.

I believe this goal is achievable. I see myself accomplishing it. I know the activities required to produce the result.

I will take disciplined, persistent and inspired action every day. I refuse to allow temporary setbacks, rejection, fear or doubt to determine my future.

I will continue learning, adjusting and moving forward until the goal is accomplished.
8Use the Mastermind
Napoleon Hill Principle: The Mastermind — success grows faster when people combine knowledge, encouragement, accountability and experience. Don't isolate yourself.
9Expect Obstacles Before They Arrive
Principle: Persistence — "Temporary defeat will cause me to adjust my plan, not abandon my goal."
10The Daily 5-Minute Reset
Do every morning. Do not wait until you "feel ready" — action creates momentum.
