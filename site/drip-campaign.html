The 7 Messages — Full Scripts
Day 0 · Immediate
Email 1 of 7
{{fname}}, something valuable for you — no strings attached
Welcome message + brochure delivery. Establishes trust, sets expectations for the series, positions you as an educator not a salesperson.
Email Education
▼
{{fname}} {{fname}} {{brochure_link}} {{agent_name}} {{agent_phone}}
Why this works: Lead with value, not a pitch. You're positioning yourself as the person who educated them — not the person who sold them something. The brochure does the heavy lifting of explaining every concept. Your job in this email is just to get them to read it.

Key phrase: "No strings attached" — removes the guard immediately.

Tone: Warm, personal, curious. Not corporate. Not salesy. Like a knowledgeable friend sharing something they found valuable.
Day 2 · SMS
Message 2 of 7
Quick SMS check-in — did you get a chance to read it?
Casual text to confirm they received the guide and open a two-way conversation. Creates a personal connection before the educational emails begin.
SMS Engagement
▼
Why SMS on Day 2: A text feels more personal than email. It invites a reply — and replies are gold. Even a "yes I read it" or "not yet" tells you where they are.

If they reply "yes": Ask what concept was most interesting to them. Their answer tells you exactly what to focus your conversations on.

If they reply "not yet": Say "No worries, take your time! I'll share a highlight from it in a few days." Then continue the sequence.
Day 4 · Email
Email 3 of 7
The life insurance feature that could save your family $200,000
Deep dive on living benefits — the most misunderstood and emotionally powerful concept in financial services. Personal story format.
Email Living Benefits Story-Based
▼
The emotional hook: Living benefits are a "fear-based" concept — but in a protective, empowering way. People fear getting sick more than they fear dying. This email taps directly into that.

The anger moment: "The part that should make you angry" creates a villain (bad policies, uninformed agents) and positions you as the person who knows better and will protect them.

First soft CTA: "Would you be open to a 20-minute conversation?" — not "buy this policy." Lower friction.
Day 7 · Email
Email 4 of 7
How I'm paying zero taxes on $1.2 million in retirement
Tax-free retirement strategies — IUL vs 401(k) showdown. The most "aha moment" email in the sequence. High share rate.
Email Tax Strategy High Engagement
▼
Why this email performs: The subject line creates curiosity ("zero taxes on $1.2 million") without being clickbait — because it's actually true and you explain it in the email.

The uncertainty hook: Opening with "do you know what tax rate you'll pay?" makes them realize they can't answer — creating immediate anxiety that the email resolves.

Stronger CTA here: Link to the schedule page for a financial review — the education has warmed them up enough for a slightly harder ask.
Day 10 · Email
Email 5 of 7
The $7,000 account the IRS can never touch
Roth IRA deep dive with specific numbers. Addresses common objections (income limits, backdoor Roth). Very high response rate.
Email Roth IRA
▼
The compound interest table: Showing the actual numbers ($97K, $287K, $707K, $1.5M) creates an immediate visual of what they're potentially leaving on the table. Numbers are more persuasive than concepts.

Objection handling built in: Addresses the "I make too much" objection proactively, then introduces the Backdoor Roth — this keeps high earners engaged rather than self-qualifying out.
Day 14 · Email
Email 6 of 7
How to guarantee $3,000/month for life — no matter what the market does
Annuities and private pensions explained with real numbers. Addresses the #1 retirement fear: outliving your money.
Email Annuities Private Pensions
▼
The fear opener: "Running out of money before running out of life" is deeply relatable and immediately creates urgency — without being manipulative, because it's genuinely a real risk.

Real numbers work: Specific example ($200K deposit, $1,800–$2,400/month at 65) makes the abstract concrete. People can picture themselves in that scenario.
Day 21 · SMS
Message 7 of 7 — The Close
Personal text — ready to put together your actual plan?
Final personal SMS. By now they've been educated on 5 major concepts. This message converts education into action. Direct but warm.
SMS Conversion CTA
▼
Why this works as the final message: You reference all the education ("life insurance, tax-free retirement, Roth IRAs, private pensions") — reminding them of the value you've delivered. Then you ask a question that invites conversation ("what resonated most?") rather than just pushing for a sale. The schedule link is there but it's not the main CTA — the question is. This consistently generates the highest reply rates.
The question strategy: Asking "what resonated most?" does two things — it re-engages someone who may have been passively reading, and it tells you exactly where they are mentally. Their answer is your opening for the next conversation.

If no reply after Day 21: That's okay. Let them marinate. Follow up again in 30 days with something new. Timing matters more than persistence.

The 6 Recruiting Messages — Full Scripts
Day 0 — Immediately
Email 1: Here's Your Video + The Opportunity
Delivers the video link and frames the income opportunity. Warm, personal, curious — not a pitch. Sets up the next email naturally.
EmailIncludes Video Link
Why this email works: The prospect already showed intent by filling out the form. This email rewards that intent immediately with the video and frames the opportunity in plain, honest language.

Variables to set in Make.com:
• {{video_link}} = https://agent.dynastybuildersapp.com/opportunity.html?agent={{referringAgent}}
• {{booking_link}} = https://agent.dynastybuildersapp.com/schedule-1on1.html?agent={{referringAgent}}&type=ri
• {{agent_name}} = agent's full name from your Make.com data store
• {{agent_phone}} = agent's phone number

Do not: pitch specific products in this email. The goal is to get them to watch the video and reply.
Day 1
SMS: Did you watch it?
Brief personal text. Creates a reply — any reply — which tells you where they are and warms the relationship before the next email.
SMS
The reply is the goal. A "not yet" reply is just as valuable as "yes I watched it." Either way you know where they are.

If they say "not yet" → reply: "No worries, take your time! Shoot me a message when you do and I'll answer any questions."
If they say "yes" → call immediately if possible, or reply: "Awesome! What stood out to you? Happy to jump on a quick call."
Day 3
Email 2: The Income Ceiling Problem
Addresses the #1 pain point of job-seekers and W-2 earners — trading time for money with no upside. Positions PHP as the solution.
Email
The income ceiling concept: This email works because it names the frustration before offering the solution. Don't lead with PHP — lead with their problem.

The override explanation is the most important paragraph. Most people have never heard of override income. Once they understand it, the math is obvious and compelling.

The path list makes the abstract concrete. "Month 1: write 3 applications" is something they can picture. Use real numbers from your own experience if possible.
Day 6
Email 3: What a Typical Week Looks Like
Removes mystery and objection about "what do you actually do." A day-in-the-life email that makes the business feel accessible and real.
Email
Remove the mystery: The biggest barrier to recruiting is "I don't know what this actually involves." This email answers that directly with a concrete schedule.

The 20-hour week framing addresses the time objection before it's raised. Most people assume this requires quitting their job. It doesn't — and saying so clearly removes a major barrier.

"Treat it like a business from day one" pre-qualifies. You want people who resonate with that — not people looking for a passive income button.
Day 10
Email 4: The 90-Day Path to $5K/Month
Income math made concrete. Shows exactly how a new agent gets from $0 to $5K/month in their first 90 days following the system.
Email
Concrete math closes hesitant people. Vague income claims create skepticism. Showing the exact arithmetic — even with modest assumptions — builds credibility because it's transparent and traceable.

The "does it change anything" close is powerful because it shifts the frame from "can I make money at this" to "what would it mean if I did." It's emotionally resonant without being manipulative.

Include your own results if you have them: "In my first 90 days I wrote X applications and earned $Y" is more powerful than any hypothetical.
Day 14
SMS: Personal Close — Book the Interview
Final personal touch. By now they've been educated across 4 emails. This SMS is a direct, warm ask to get on a call. No more nurturing — time to book.
SMS
"I think you'd be great at this" is one of the most powerful sentences in recruiting. It's a genuine compliment that creates social obligation — they don't want to let you down by not even taking a call.

Transparency works: "I've been sharing info... I want to be straight with you" signals honesty and builds trust at the moment you're asking for something.

After this SMS: If they don't book within 48 hours, call them directly. The sequence is designed to warm them up for a live conversation — not replace it.
