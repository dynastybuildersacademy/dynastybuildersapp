Where are you living from, most days?

This is a short self-assessment based on a model of consciousness levels — from contracted states like shame and fear, up through courage, to expanded states like acceptance and love.

Answer for the last 2–4 weeks, not just today. There's no scoring to perform well on — the point is an honest snapshot of your baseline, and a next step that actually fits where you are.
