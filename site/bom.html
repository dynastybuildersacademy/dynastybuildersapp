📝 BOM Invite Scripts

Use these when inviting prospects — keep it short and create curiosity, not information overload.

Text Message Invite:
"Hey [Name], I've been meaning to reach out. I'm doing something in the financial services space and I wanted to invite you to an info session I'm hosting with some colleagues. Zero pressure — just sharing what we do. This [Tuesday 7PM / Saturday 10:30AM]. Can you make it?"
Confirmation Call (Day Before):
"Hey [Name], just confirming for tomorrow! We start at [time] — the session usually wraps in about 90 minutes. You'll be hearing from some top producers in our field. Excited for you to see this. You're still coming?"
Same-Night Follow-Up (After BOM):
"Hey [Name], glad you came tonight! What stood out most to you? [Listen] — Great. I'd love to get you started. When are you free this week to sit down for 20 minutes?"
