⚡ How to connect Zapier
1. Go to zapier.com → Create Zap → Trigger: Webhooks by Zapier → Catch Hook
2. Copy the webhook URL Zapier gives you
3. Paste it into the field for the sequence below
4. Click Save & Test — we'll fire a test payload so Zapier can map the fields
5. Build your Zapier actions (email, SMS, CRM, Slack, etc.)
6. Turn on the Zap
